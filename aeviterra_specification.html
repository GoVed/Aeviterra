The Century-Scale Autonomous Virtual World: Architecture, Game Systems & Deterministic Execution Substrate
Executive Protocol Overview The Aeviterra Protocol defines a sovereign, peer-operated virtual universe engineered to persist across centuries without centralized server infrastructure or corporate dependencies. The architecture guarantees:

1. Pure Deterministic Digital Physics: Software-emulated Q64.64 fixed-point arithmetic with exact 256-bit wide intermediates and zero floating-point divergence across CPU architectures (x86-64, ARM64, RISC-V).
2. Sovereign Multi-Shard Consensus: HotStuff-variant BFT with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL), BLS threshold committee shuffling, and dynamic swept-volume halos with octree split/merge hysteresis.
3. Leased State Enclaves with Single-Use Settlement Nullifiers: Provable offline wilderness exploration using zkVM execution receipts, single-use nullifiers derived from LeaseID alone, and a 200-tick reclamation grace barrier eliminating timeline forking.
4. Classless Action MMO Systems: Nine weapon families, stamina-gated combat with i-frame dodge rolls, crowd-control diminishing returns, closed integer mass conservation, and tier-isolated loot pity counters.

PART I: THE PLAYABLE GAME DESIGN

Aeviterra treats player agency, rich social dynamics, and fluid action combat as first-class architectural constraints. Security bounds ensure the world cannot be cheated; game design ensures it remains deep and engaging.

1. World & Content Geography

  • Procedural-from-Seed, Authored-by-Canon: Continental terrain, mountain ranges, river networks, and mineral distributions are generated locally by each client from an immutable 32-byte Genesis Seed via deterministic fixed-point noise functions (zero persistent storage overhead). Historic landmarks, keeps, and monuments are established by Canon Records—cryptographically signed ledger entries.
  • Finite, Bounded Active Surface: The explorable surface is strictly capped at a 4,096 × 4,096 chunk grid (~524,000 square kilometers, a continent-scale landmass). Infinitely expanding procedural terrain is prohibited to maintain player density, territorial politics, and closed economic mass conservation. Expansion occurs exclusively via scheduled world epochs.
  • Ecological Biomes:
    • Temperate Heartlands: Safe zones, beginner settlements, agrarian plots, and craft workshops. Protected from open PvP.
    • Frontier Wilds: Rich mineral and lumber deposits, player-founded settlements, and flag-based opt-in PvP.
    • Deep Wilds: High-risk, high-yield territory. Open PvP with full-loot on carried currency and raw materials (equipped soulbound gear is never dropped).
    • The Underdeep: Procedural subterranean labyrinths containing eldritch materials, high-tier crafting reagents, and raid bosses.
  • Deterministic Seasons & World Clocks: Seasons advance on the global tick clock. Winter freezes river crossings and slows siegecraft; spring accelerates agricultural yields and mob migration.

2. Character Systems & Classless Buildcraft

  • Nine Weapon & Ability Families: Rather than rigid character classes, players equip a Primary and Secondary weapon family from: Blade (slashing/counters), Spear (reach/pierce), Bow (range/kiting), Wand/Prism (arcane burst/deflection), Staff (elemental control), Shield (poise/blocking), Shadow (mobility/criticals), Song (auras/support buffs), and Tinker (deployable turrets, traps, siegecraft, alchemy).
  • Core Attributes: Might (physical power/carrying capacity), Finesse (attack cadence/crit chance), Resolve (stamina pool/blocking stability), Wit (ability efficiency/trap craft), and Spirit (restoration/buff potency). Attributes derive from worn equipment and training allocations; respec is low-cost to encourage build experimentation.
  • Action Combat Mechanics:
    • Instant Responsiveness: Client prediction renders instant swings, blocks, and rolls. Consensus evaluates authoritative hitboxes. Rollback is display-only: finalized combat outcomes are never rewritten.
    • Stamina-Gated Movesets: Abilities have no arbitrary cooldown timers; all actions consume stamina. A skilled player balances stamina, i-frame dodge rolls (10 ticks / 200ms invulnerability), and directional blocking (mitigating 70% damage).
    • Crowd-Control Diminishing Returns: Stun, freeze, and stagger effects suffer strict diminishing returns (100% → 50% → 25% → 0% duration / immunity), preventing stun-lock degeneracy.
  • Cosmetic-Only Prestige Currency: Titles, cloaks, heraldic crests, and masterwork weapon glows represent player prestige. Prestige confers zero combat stats and cannot be weaponized.

3. Economy, Crafting & Settlements

  • Dual-Coin Currency System:
    • Marks (Soft Currency): Minted by mob bounties and beginner quests; drained by gear repairs, municipal transport taxes, and food upkeep.
    • Sovereigns (Hard Currency): Minted strictly through endgame territory conquest, high-tier trade caravans, and seasonal events. Used for keep upgrades and grand auction houses.
    • Zero NPC Infinite Gold: Every coin in circulation originates from an explicit, monitored faucet and leaves through certified sinks. Total currency supply is tracked per epoch.
    • Liquidity Demurrage: Stagnant Sovereign vaults uncirculated for >180 days incur an algorithmic 5% demurrage decay redirected into regional public infrastructure funds, preventing cartel hoarding.
  • Crafting Centrality: Best-in-slot gear is craftable by player artisans, not dropped exclusively by raids. Raid bosses drop unique crafting catalysts and cosmetic modifiers, ensuring crafters remain central to the world economy.
  • Homesteads & Settlement Governance: Housing is a guaranteed right. Every player can claim a homestead plot in a chartered settlement and construct buildings from typed semantic structural primitives. Settlements elect mayors, levy local trade taxes, and construct public defenses. Mayoral voting requires proven economic tenure (30 days residency + tax bond) to prevent Sybil bot takeovers.

5. Core Cryptographic Subsystems

5.1. Validator Admission & Verifiable Shard Shuffling

  1. Bonded Operator Federation: Admitted federation of 30–100 independent operators across diverse bare-metal and cloud providers posting slashed storage bonds.
  2. Verifiable Shard Committee Shuffling: For spatial chunk C = (x, y, z) and epoch E, the authoritative consensus committee of size n = 3f + 1 is sampled deterministically using the BLS threshold beacon seed:
    Committee(C, E) = Sample( ValidatorPool, H(σE || C) )
    Players inside chunk C never vote on their own actions.
  3. HotStuff-Variant BFT with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL): Proposals proceed through Prepare, Pre-commit, and Commit phases. Every commit certificate requires ≥ 2f + 1 distinct validator signatures persisted to disk before broadcast.

5.2. Leased State Enclaves with Single-Use Settlement Nullifiers

  1. State Lease Checkout: An avatar entering unobserved wilderness checks out an exclusive lease from the shard committee:
    LeaseID = H(ActorID || Rstart || Tickgrant || Salt)
    The committee locks the avatar's state in the Sparse Merkle Tree, assigning a tick horizon Tmax, grace barrier Δgrace, and entropy seed Sentropy.
  2. Deterministic Offline Execution: The client executes the Wasm/zkVM kernel locally. Inputs form an unbroken cryptographic hash chain:
    C0 = LeaseID,     Ci = H(Ci−1 || Actioni)
  3. Single-Use Nullifier & Grace Barrier: Settlement nullifier is derived strictly from `LeaseID`:
    N = H(LeaseID || "AEVITERRA_LEASE_NULLIFIER_V4_SETTLE")
    Conflicting timeline proofs share the exact same nullifier; the first timeline burns it and the second is permanently rejected. A lease cannot be reclaimed as expired until tick Tmax + Δgrace, superseding expiration if a valid proof arrives.

5.3. Dynamic Swept-Volume Halos & Durable 2PC

  • Dynamic Swept Margin: Halo bounds adapt dynamically to velocity, acceleration, and rollback horizons:
    Rhalo = Rinteract + vmax · Δtrollback + ½ amax · (Δtrollback)2 + εnumerical
    Fast projectiles declare continuous swept bounding capsules AABB(t0 → t1) covering all intersecting chunk volumes.
  • Octree Split/Merge Hysteresis: Shard capacity is governed by an 80% split threshold, 20% merge threshold, and a 300-tick minimum lifetime barrier, preventing boundary oscillation churn.
  • Durable Journal Decisions: Transactions declare write-sets and persist prepare locks. Transactions resolve strictly through a consensus-certified CertifiedJournalDecision (Commit or Abort) recorded in the Control Journal.

5.4. Irrevocable Commit-Before-Entropy Randomness Pipeline

  1. Action Commitment (Tick T): Action intent is submitted and costs (stamina, ammo, weapon durability) are debited immediately. An assigned beacon round Rtarget = Rcurrent + ΔR is locked.
  2. Deterministic Threshold Resolution: At round Rtarget, the committee emits threshold BLS signature σR. The outcome resolves via unbiased counter-mode rejection sampling:
    Eevent = H("AEVITERRA_EVENT_RNG_V1" || action_id || Rtarget || σR)
    Because costs were debited at Step 1, player disconnects do not pause resolution. Selective abort is mathematically impossible.

5.5. Pure Semantic Generative Asset Grammar

Consensus state contains zero binary meshes or texture images. Assets exist purely as strongly typed mathematical tokens: 

StructuralPrimitive { style: FortifiedCitadel, material: Granite, length: 20, height: 10, thickness: 2, features: 0b101 } // 48 bytes
HeraldryToken { primary_field: Azure, charge: LionRampant, charge_color: Or } // 8 bytes
EquipmentGrammar { item_class_id: Broadsword, primary_material: DamascusSteel, mass_grams: 1400 } // 32 bytes

Clients render photorealistic geometry procedurally from verified grammar rules. Steganographic carrier capacity is reduced to zero, state footprint is compressed by 99.99%, and node operators bear zero media liability.
